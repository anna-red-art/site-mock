Ohanami Bar

Year
2025
Size
F8  455 × 380mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic

A spring afternoon. The cherry blossom bar beneath the blooming sakura is bustling as always.

A flamingo who adores all things pink has opened a bar under the weeping cherry tree.

An owl with a bottle keep takes their usual seat.

Drawn by the pleasant season, cheerful friends arrive one after another.

Conversations are in full bloom too — and spring is far from over.
