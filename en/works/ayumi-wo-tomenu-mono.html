The Relentless Walker
歩みを止めぬ者

Year
2026
Size
F30  727 × 910mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic Leather

An entity that quietly keeps moving through the city.

One work depicting the "symbol of time" — advancing while constantly changing its form.

This work incorporates leather scraps born from the process of bag-making. By layering pieces that have completed their role, I expressed the appearance of a concrete jungle in constant renewal and metabolism.

Like an African elephant forging a new path through the forest, knocking down trees as it goes — the end of one role is an inevitability that connects to the next.

Inheriting what was passed down from those who came before, turning it with our own hands, and passing it on to the next generation. An endless story of circulation continuing without pause, from past to present to future.
