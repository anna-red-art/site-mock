Until That Time Comes
時が来るまで

Year
2024
Size
F30  910 × 727mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic

The story of a chameleon who never gave up hope for the future.

Born into difficult circumstances, she was mistreated, had her freedom taken away, and felt deep loneliness — yet she lived on with strength.

"Every twenty years, the eagle flips this hourglass upside down. When that moment comes, I'll hold on tight and travel to a free future world."

"I'll meet wonderful friends, see a wide world, and live my own life."

Without killing her spirit, enduring hardships with patience, clinging to the clock, she waited. Until that time came.
