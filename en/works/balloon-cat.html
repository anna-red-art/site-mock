About This Work

A white cat quietly gazes at the sky, holding a red balloon. This painting explores the small touches of fantasy hidden within everyday life—a central theme in my work.

Born from the simple question, "Where would a cat go if it had a balloon?", this original artwork combines animal motifs with fantasy elements, inviting viewers to create their own narrative.

Creative Background

The inspiration came from a moment I witnessed on a street corner—a stray cat showing interest in a balloon dropped by a child. In that instant, an ordinary scene transformed into something magical.

Using layers of acrylic paint on canvas, I worked to capture the soft light and the weightless feeling of the balloon. This piece represents my ongoing exploration of how fantasy art can emerge from the simplest observations of daily life.
