Cycle

Year
2026
Size
Ø300mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic Leather

An uninterrupted, ever-repeating flow.

Just as water and air circulate, materials too change form and journey onward. Matter takes a trip.

Choosing a circular canvas to represent the endless cycle of form, a vivid light-blue leather is incorporated as part of crashing waves — expressing a return to the circle of circulation.

Inspired by the colors and textures of materials that had become waste, this work explores the ongoing circulation of matter.
