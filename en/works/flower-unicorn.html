The Florist Unicorn
花屋のユニコーン

Year
2021
Size
S12  606 × 606mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic

A unicorn that travels the world selling flowers gathered from across the globe.

Having heard the rumor and eagerly waited, forest animals of all generations and species gather together.

Coming to buy a favorite flower, to share a conversation — a little extra-special moment in the familiar forest.

Inspired by the artist's own experience with street performance.
