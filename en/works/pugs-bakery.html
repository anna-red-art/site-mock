Pug's Bakery

Year
2025
Size
F8  455 × 380mm
Material
Wood panel Acrylic

Morning preparations at the bakery before opening time.

The pug manager climbs a ladder to inspect the products! The part-timer arranges price tags while fighting the urge to sneak a taste.

Today too, a delicious day begins for the customers.

One day, the pug baker caught a glimpse of their own tail in the mirror and thought, "If this were baked, it would surely be delicious!" — and inspiration struck...

...and that, or so the legend goes, is how the now-famous signature bagel was (or wasn't) born.
