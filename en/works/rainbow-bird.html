Rainbow Messenger

Year

Medium

About This Work

A bird with rainbow-colored wings flying freely through the post-rain sky. This fantasy artwork celebrates hope and freedom as its central themes.

Like a rainbow appearing after rain, hope always follows hardship. I've channeled this message into the vibrant form of a bird—an animal motif symbolizing freedom and release, bringing lightness to the viewer's heart.

Creative Background

The inspiration came from witnessing a bird cross beneath a rainbow in a park after rain. In that fleeting moment, it looked as if the rainbow had painted the bird with its colors.

Using the brilliant pigments of acrylic paint on canvas, I layered the seven colors—red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet—across the bird's wings. This original artwork speaks through color, expressing the fantasy hidden within everyday scenes and the interplay of light and hue in a magical world.
