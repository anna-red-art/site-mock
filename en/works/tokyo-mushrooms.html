TOKYO mushrooms

Year
2022

If Tokyo's humidity were 75%, perhaps all the buildings would be mushrooms.

Maybe even Rainbow Bridge would be made of veiled lady mushrooms.

And the cats living there would surely climb all the way to the moon.

Inspired by the view from the spherical observation deck in Odaiba.
