City of Melbourne

Year
2018
Size
F6  410 × 318mm
Material
Paper Acrylic

A realistic depiction of a sunset in Melbourne, Australia.

For over a decade, every summer was spent performing street shows in this city.

A place where friendships were forged, lives were shared, and challenges were taken on.

People of many nationalities and cultures, all gazing up at the same sky as it filled with countless colors.

The time spent meeting and talking with so many people, until sundown, remains an irreplaceable part of life.
