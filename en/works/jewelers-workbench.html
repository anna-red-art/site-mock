Jeweler's workbench

Year
2023
Size
P10  410 × 530mm
Material
Canvas Acrylic

This is a bespoke jeweler specializing in stones native to Australia.

A diverse cast of animals on working holidays work diligently together today at their dedicated benches.

Inspired by life in Melbourne — a city where people of many backgrounds coexist — and by the shared workbench of the artist's companions during that time.
