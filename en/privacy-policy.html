Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2026

ANNA RED (the "Site") handles personal information with care. This page explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information entered in the contact form (name, email address, message, etc.)

  • Access log data such as IP address, date and time, and browser information

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and communicate with you
  • To handle requests related to artworks, exhibitions, media, and collaborations

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties without consent except when required by law. External services may be used only as needed for operations such as email delivery and hosting.

4. Security

We take reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, leakage, and tampering of personal data.

5. External Links

This site includes links to external services such as Shop, Instagram, X, and YouTube. Data handling on those services is governed by each provider's own policies.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact info@annaredart.com.
